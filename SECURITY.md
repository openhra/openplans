# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems. Use this repository's private
vulnerability reporting (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability"), or reach the maintainers
through the contact section of <https://www.openhra.com/#contact>.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the version or commit you tested. We will
acknowledge the report, keep you informed, and credit you in the fix unless you prefer otherwise.

## Scope

OpenPlans is self-hosted. The operator of each installation is responsible for its deployment,
credentials and network exposure. Services bind to `127.0.0.1` only by default.
